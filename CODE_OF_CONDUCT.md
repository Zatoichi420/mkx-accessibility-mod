# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is an accessibility project. We want it to be a welcoming place for
everyone — blind and sighted, first-time contributors and veterans, regardless of
disability, age, body, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexuality.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Remember that many people here use screen readers — describe images you post,
  and don't rely on colour or layout alone to make a point.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.
- Attaching copyrighted game files (installers, extracted assets, cracks) after
  being asked not to.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a private security advisory on the repository, or by
contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed
and handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions
that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone
for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
